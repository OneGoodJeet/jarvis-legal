Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Who we are

Jarvis ("the app", "we") is made by Big Yesh LLC, Arizona, United States. Questions about this policy or your data: bigyeshllc@shatisand.com.

The short version

  • Your health, fitness, habits, tasks and notes are yours. We use them only to run the app for you.
  • We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never share it with data brokers.
  • Health data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, never shared with third parties for those purposes, and never stored in iCloud.
  • The app has no ads, no ad trackers and no third-party analytics.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

Account. You sign in with Apple. We receive a user ID from Apple and, if you choose to share it, your email address (which may be an Apple private relay address). We don't receive your Apple password.

Health and fitness data you allow. With your permission, the app reads from Apple Health things such as weight, steps, sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate and workouts. When you finish a workout you logged in the app, it can save that workout to Apple Health with only its type and its start and end times (no calories, heart rate or other readings). Deleting the workout in the app removes it from Apple Health too. You choose exactly which types to share in the Health permission screen and can change it any time in iOS Settings.

What you enter. Workouts and sets, habits and check-ins, tasks, goals, food logs, notes and other things you type into the app.

Daily summaries from other permissions (optional).

  • Calendar: a summary of the day's events so they appear next to your plan. We never change your calendar.
  • Motion & fitness: daily active minutes and stairs climbed.
  • Location: only daily totals such as time away from home and whether you travelled. Exact places stay on your iPhone and are never uploaded.
  • Photos: the app shows photos from a given day on your iPhone. Photos are never uploaded.

Purchases. Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We don't receive your payment details. We receive only whether your subscription is active.

Technical data. Basic records needed to keep the service working and secure, such as when your data last synced and which app version you run.

How we use it

Only to provide the app to you: to show your dashboards, scores, trends and history; to sync your data across reinstalls and devices; to send the reminders you turn on (these are local notifications, scheduled on your iPhone); and to keep the service secure. We don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored on your iPhone. When you're signed in, a copy is stored in your private account on our database provider, Supabase, hosted on Amazon Web Services in the United States. Each account can access only its own data; this is enforced in the database itself. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our hosting provider.

If you turn on the optional iCloud Drive archive, the app writes a daily text summary to your own iCloud Drive. That archive never contains health data.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers that run the app on our behalf, under contracts that limit them to doing that:

  • Supabase (database, authentication and server functions)
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, subscriptions, notifications)

We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't share it for advertising. We may disclose data if the law requires it, and we'll tell you if we're allowed to.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Items you delete are removed from the app straight away and permanently erased from our servers within 90 days. When you delete your account, your account and all synced data are erased immediately.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account. This erases your account and all synced data, revokes Sign in with Apple, and clears the app's data on that iPhone. It doesn't touch data stored in Apple Health.
  • Export your data: Settings → Data & export.
  • Change permissions: in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security, or the Health app → Sharing.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us at bigyeshllc@shatisand.com; we'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Jarvis isn't directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent where you live, if that is higher, such as 16 in parts of the EU), and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Health information

Jarvis is a wellness and fitness app, not a medical device. Scores such as readiness, recovery, strain and sleep are estimates to help you notice trends. They don't diagnose or treat any condition.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the effective date above, and for significant changes we'll tell you in the app before they take effect.
