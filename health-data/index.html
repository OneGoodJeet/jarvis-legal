Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026 (consent version 5: the heartbeat series and RMSSD, Apple's heart notifications, workout routes, the health profile backup, notes for the coach and what Tempest writes to Apple Health added; breathing disturbances, foods you avoid and the coach's view of today added November 11; sharing steps in challenges and the consent record added November 9; date of birth and height from Apple Health added November 8)

This policy explains how Big Yesh LLC ("we") handles consumer health data in the Tempest app. It is written for the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada's consumer health data law (SB 370), Connecticut's consumer health data rules and similar laws, and it applies to every Tempest user. It adds to our Privacy Policy.

The consumer health data we collect

  • From Apple Health, only the types you allow: weight and body composition (body fat, lean mass, BMI), steps, distance, flights climbed, active energy, exercise and stand minutes, sleep and sleep stages, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, walking heart rate, heart rate recovery, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, VO2 max, sleeping wrist temperature, breathing disturbances during sleep (from Apple Watch), blood pressure, blood glucose, mindful minutes, time in daylight, sound exposure levels, workouts, height, the beat-to-beat heartbeat series your Apple Watch records and Apple's RMSSD readings (used to measure your heart rate variability overnight), the heart notifications your Apple Watch has sent you (irregular rhythm, high heart rate, low heart rate and possible hypertension), the route of an outdoor workout recorded by your Apple Watch, and the sex and date of birth recorded in Health. Heart notifications are shown as dated entries, exactly as your Apple Watch recorded them in Apple Health. Tempest doesn't work them out, doesn't change them and doesn't diagnose anything from them. They stay on your iPhone. Your date of birth is used only on your iPhone to work out your age for your calorie targets; it is not stored in your account. This includes readings that a watch, ring, scale, blood pressure cuff or glucose sensor writes to Apple Health through its own app.
  • From your iPhone's Motion & Fitness data, if you allow it: daily active minutes and stairs climbed.
  • What you log: workouts and sets, food and nutrition (calories, protein and other nutrients), nutrition targets, habits and check-ins (which can relate to health, such as sleep or meditation), and goals. If you set them, the foods you avoid (allergies, intolerances and diets such as vegetarian, vegan, halal or kosher). They stay on your iPhone, are never copied to your account, and are used only to shape the food coach's answers. Your health profile, if you set one: a status (pregnant, postpartum, taking a heart-rate medicine, or unwell, with the days it started and ended) and the sex you choose. It is used only to keep your training and calorie targets safe. It is backed up to your private account so a new iPhone keeps those safety holds. Notes you ask the coach to remember (for example an injury, or when you train). They stay on your iPhone, are never copied to your account, and are read only by the coach on your iPhone. Your weight, if you log a weigh-in in the app, and your mood, if you log it. Caffeine and alcohol are part of the food you log.
  • What the app works out from that: scores and trends such as Recovery, Energy, Strain, your sleep score and sleep need, stress and calm minutes, body age and personal records. (Earlier versions called the morning score Readiness.)

Workout routes. If you allow it in the Health permission screen, the page for a run, walk, ride or hike recorded by your Apple Watch shows its route on a map. The route is read from Apple Health when you open that page. Tempest never saves it, never copies it to your account and never sends it to us. The map is drawn by Apple Maps, so Apple receives the area the map shows, under Apple's privacy policy.

Apart from showing that route on your iPhone, we don't collect precise location, and we never use a geofence around any health care service. Fast food near you uses your location once, only when you tap "Use my location", to search Apple Maps; it isn't saved or sent to us.

What Tempest writes to Apple Health

Tempest writes to Apple Health only with the write permission you give in the Health permission screen, and only these things:

  • A lifting workout you log is recorded in Apple Health as a workout, with its type, its start and end times and the active calories your iPhone counts while it runs. If a heart rate sensor is paired, your heart rate shows in the logger while you train. The live heart rate and calories are not copied to your account.
  • If you turn on Save food to Apple Health: the calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, caffeine, sodium, sugars and saturated fat of the food you log.
  • A weigh-in you log in the app is saved as your weight.
  • Water you log is saved as water.
  • A timed habit, such as meditation, is saved as mindful minutes.

Deleting the workout or the food in Tempest removes what it wrote. Once something is in Apple Health, Apple Health and the other apps you have allowed can read it.

Where it comes from

From you: Apple Health and Motion & Fitness on your iPhone, with the permissions you grant, and what you type into the app. We don't buy health data or get it from anyone else.

Why we collect and use it

Only to provide the app you asked for: to show your training, nutrition, sleep and recovery, calculate your scores and trends, show you how your trends compare week to week and month to month, send the reminders and trend alerts you turn on (worked out on your iPhone), and, if you turn on the food coach, give it a weekly summary of your trends, today's call (push, steady or back off, never the score behind it), last night's sleep and the foods you avoid, all on your iPhone, so its suggestions fit how you're sleeping, recovering and training, sync what you log, your health profile, your daily Motion summary and the scores the app works out to your private account so they survive a new phone (the readings themselves from Apple Health stay on your iPhone and are not copied to your account), and keep the service secure. If you set a goal or a habit to track itself from Apple Health (for example steps, sleep, mindful minutes or time in daylight), the goal's progress number or the habit's tick is saved with it in your account. The readings behind it are not. If you join a step challenge in Friends & challenges, we also use your daily step totals for that challenge's days to show you and the other people in it how everyone is pacing. We never use it for advertising, never sell it, and never use it to train AI models.

Your consent

Tempest asks for your consent before it collects any consumer health data. The consent screen appears when you set up the app, separate from the Terms and this policy, and you have to tick the box yourself. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Your data → Health data consent. When you withdraw, the app stops reading and syncing health data straight away and asks again before collecting any more. We keep a record of when you give or withdraw consent (the date, the version of the consent and which you did), so we can show what you agreed to. Data already in your account stays until you delete it, which you can do in Settings → Delete account or by asking us.

We won't share consumer health data with anyone other than the processors listed below without asking for your separate consent first, and we will never sell it.

Sharing steps in challenges. Friends & challenges is off until you turn it on. The first time you join or create a step challenge, a separate screen asks whether you agree to share your daily step totals with the people in each challenge you join. It explains exactly what is shared, and nothing is shared until you agree. We store the version of that screen and the time you agreed with each challenge you join. To stop sharing, leave the challenge (its step totals are deleted unless another challenge of yours covers those days) or turn Friends & challenges off in Settings (all of them are deleted).

Who we share it with

We share consumer health data only with processors that run the app on our behalf, under contracts that limit them to doing that:

  • Supabase (with Amazon Web Services in the United States): stores your synced data (not your Apple Health readings) in your private account and runs sign-in.
  • Apple: Apple Health, Sign in with Apple and subscriptions run on Apple's systems, and Apple Maps draws the map under a workout route.
  • Anthropic (only on iPhones without Apple Intelligence, and only if you agree to it separately; on iPhones with Apple Intelligence the food coach runs on your iPhone and sends nothing): when you ask the coach something, it receives your question, the recent conversation and a summary of what you logged in the app (food, calories and macros for the last two weeks, nutrition targets, goal, calorie floor and workouts logged in the app this week) to write the answer. Never your Apple Health readings or the trend summary the on-device coach reads. It keeps requests only for a limited time for safety monitoring and doesn't train models on them. The coach asks for this separate consent before anything is sent, and you can turn it off in Settings → Your data → Food coach.

People you choose: the members of a step challenge you join see your daily step total for each of its days and the average worked out from them. They see nothing else from Apple Health, and no one outside the challenge sees your steps.

Your Apple Watch: if you install the Tempest Watch app, your iPhone sends it today's summary, your open workout, your routines and your recent foods directly. Nothing extra goes through our servers.

Food searches and barcode lookups go to Open Food Facts with only the words or barcode you entered, never any of your health data. We have no affiliates that receive consumer health data.

Your rights

  • Know and access: confirm whether we collect your consumer health data, see it, and get the list of every third party and affiliate we have shared it with (today, only the processors above). Settings → Data & export gives you a copy any time.
  • Withdraw consent: Settings → Your data → Health data consent.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete account erases your account and all synced data at once. We also tell our processors to delete it, and it leaves their backups within 30 days.
  • No discrimination: we won't treat you differently for using these rights.

To make a request by email, write to bigyeshllc@shatisand.com from the address on your account. We'll confirm it's you, respond within 45 days, and may extend that once by 45 days if we tell you why. If we decline, you can appeal by replying with "Appeal" in the subject; we'll answer within 45 days and explain why. If your appeal is denied, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General (atg.wa.gov), the Nevada Attorney General (ag.nv.gov), or the attorney general where you live.

How we protect it

Encryption in transit and at rest, database rules that let each account reach only its own data, and access limited to the people who need it to run the service. If a breach affects your health data, we'll tell you and the regulators the law requires.

Changes

If we change what consumer health data we collect or why, we'll update this policy and ask for your consent again in the app before collecting under the new terms.
