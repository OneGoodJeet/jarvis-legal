Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 (consent version 13: counts of which screens and features you use kept with your account, and totals of what people log; what Coach knows and your coach chats backed up to your account, and this week's Apple Health workouts read by Coach; Coach reads the food you log, photos included; Coach runs on Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, and OpenAI voices an answer read aloud; period days, State of Mind, waist, body details backed up, mood, body measurements and progress photos added)

This policy explains how Big Yesh LLC ("we") handles consumer health data in the Tempest app. It is written for the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada's consumer health data law (SB 370), Connecticut's consumer health data rules and similar laws, and it applies to every Tempest user. It adds to our Privacy Policy.

The consumer health data we collect

  • From Apple Health, only the types you allow: weight and body composition (body fat, lean mass, BMI), your waist measurement, steps, walking and running distance, cycling distance, swimming distance, flights climbed, active energy, exercise and stand minutes, sleep and sleep stages, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, walking heart rate, heart rate recovery, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, VO2 max, sleeping wrist temperature, breathing disturbances during sleep (from Apple Watch), blood pressure, blood glucose, mindful minutes, time in daylight, sound exposure levels, workouts, height, the beat-to-beat heartbeat series your Apple Watch records and Apple's RMSSD readings (used to measure your heart rate variability overnight), the route of an outdoor workout recorded by your Apple Watch and the distance readings of a run (for its splits and best times), your period days, State of Mind that you or other apps logged, Apple's own notifications from your Apple Watch, and the sex and date of birth recorded in Health. This includes readings that a watch, ring, scale, blood pressure cuff or glucose sensor writes to Apple Health through its own app. These readings stay on your iPhone and are not copied to your account, except as described below.
  • Apple's notifications from your Apple Watch: irregular rhythm, high and low heart rate, hypertension and sleep apnea. We show them as dated entries, exactly as Apple sent them and as your Apple Watch recorded them in Apple Health. Tempest never works them out, doesn't change them and doesn't diagnose anything from them. They stay on your iPhone.
  • Your period days (menstrual flow), read only. We use them only to read your wrist temperature, heart rate variability and resting heart rate in the light of your cycle. We never predict periods or fertility. Your period days are read on your iPhone and kept in its memory: never stored by Tempest, never copied to your account, never sent to the coach or to anyone.
  • State of Mind. State of Mind that you or other apps logged in Apple Health is read on your iPhone, so your patterns can compare your days by how you felt. It is never stored by Tempest or copied to your account.
  • Past food, once, when you ask. If you choose to bring in food from Apple Health, Tempest copies once the past food other apps saved there (calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat, fiber, sugars, sodium and caffeine). It then becomes part of your food log, which is backed up to your account like any food you log. It is never read on a schedule.
  • Your date of birth is used only on your iPhone to work out your age; it is not stored in your account.
  • From your iPhone's Motion & Fitness data, if you allow it: your minutes walking, running and cycling, and floors climbed, each day. We don't keep time spent driving or sitting still.
  • What you log: workouts and sets, how hard a workout felt, food and nutrition (calories, protein and other nutrients), the meals you plan, nutrition targets, habits and check-ins (which can relate to health, such as sleep or meditation), your morning check-in (how you slept and feel, when you slept if you type it, and where you're sore), and goals. Your weight, if you log a weigh-in in the app, your mood, if you tap How do you feel?, and body measurements you take with a tape (waist, chest, hips, upper arm, thigh and neck). Caffeine and alcohol are part of the food you log. If you set them, the foods you avoid (allergies, intolerances and diets such as vegetarian, vegan, halal or kosher), used only to shape the coach's answers. Notes you or the coach keep for the coach (for example an injury, or when you train). With the coach on, these, the coach's notes on your chats and your coach chats are backed up to your private account so a new iPhone has them (consent version 12); turning the coach off removes them from your account.
  • About you: if you enter them (or let Apple Health fill them in), your sex, age, height, weight and activity level, so your targets and scores come back on a new iPhone. Your date of birth stays on your iPhone. Your health profile, if you set one: a status (pregnant, postpartum, taking a heart-rate medicine, or unwell, with the days it started and ended) and the sex you choose. It is used only to keep your training and calorie targets safe. Both are backed up to your private account so a new iPhone keeps them and the safety holds.
  • Progress photos you take in the app are kept only on your iPhone, left out of iCloud backup, never uploaded or synced, and deleted when you withdraw health-data consent, sign out or delete your account. They are included in your data export.
  • What the app works out from that: scores and trends such as Energy, Strain, your sleep score and sleep need, stress and calm minutes, body age and personal records. (Earlier versions called the morning score Readiness or Recovery.) Your Energy score, sleep need and suggested bedtime are worked out on your iPhone from Apple Health and are not stored in your account. If Coach is on, today's Energy is among the things sent to write its answers.

Workout routes. If you allow it in the Health permission screen, the page for a run, walk, ride or hike recorded by your Apple Watch shows its route on a map, and a run's splits and best times. The route and the distance readings are read from Apple Health when you open that page. Tempest never saves them, never copies them to your account and never sends them to us. The map is drawn by Apple Maps, so Apple receives the area the map shows, under Apple's privacy policy.

Apart from showing that route on your iPhone, we don't collect precise location, and we never use a geofence around any health care service. Fast food near you uses your location once, only when you tap "Use my location", to search Apple Maps; it isn't saved or sent to us.

What Tempest writes to Apple Health

Tempest writes to Apple Health only with the write permission you give in the Health permission screen, and only these things:

  • A lifting workout you log is recorded in Apple Health as a workout, with its type, its start and end times and the active calories your iPhone counts while it runs (a workout you add afterwards has no calories). If a heart rate sensor is paired, your heart rate shows in the logger while you train. The live heart rate and calories are not copied to your account.
  • A cardio session you log by hand is saved as a workout with its type, its start and end times and the distance you typed (unless your Apple Watch already recorded it).
  • How hard a workout felt (Apple's workout effort score), with the workout.
  • If you turn on Save food to Apple Health: the calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, caffeine, sodium, sugars and saturated fat of the food you log, its cholesterol, vitamins (A, C, D, E, K, B1, B2, B3, B6, B12 and folate) and minerals (potassium, calcium, iron, magnesium, zinc, phosphorus, selenium, copper and manganese), and the water you log with the Water habit.
  • A weigh-in you log in the app is saved as your weight, and so is a weight history you import from another app.
  • Your mood, as State of Mind (how pleasant the moment felt, with no labels), each time you tap How do you feel?
  • Mindful minutes from the breathing minute.

Deleting the workout or the food in Tempest removes what it wrote. When you delete your account, Apple Health keeps what Tempest saved there (workouts, effort scores, food, water, weigh-ins, moods and mindful minutes) unless you turn on "Also remove workouts, food, weigh-ins, moods and mindful minutes Tempest saved to Apple Health" on the delete screen. Once something is in Apple Health, Apple Health and the other apps you have allowed can read it.

Where it comes from

From you: Apple Health and Motion & Fitness on your iPhone, with the permissions you grant, files you choose to import from other apps, and what you type into the app. We don't buy health data or get it from anyone else.

Why we collect and use it

To provide the app you asked for: to show your training, nutrition, sleep and recovery, calculate your scores and trends, show you how your trends compare week to week and month to month, judge your temperature, HRV and resting heart rate against your cycle if you log periods, compare your days by mood in your patterns, send the reminders and trend alerts you turn on (worked out on your iPhone), and, if you turn on Coach, send Anthropic the summaries listed under "Who we share it with", so Claude can write answers that fit how you're sleeping, recovering and training; to sync what you log, your health profile, the details about you, your daily Motion summary and the training numbers the app works out to your private account so they survive a new phone (the readings themselves from Apple Health stay on your iPhone and are not copied to your account); and to keep the service secure. If you set a goal or a habit to track itself from Apple Health (for example steps, sleep, mindful minutes or time in daylight), the goal's progress number or the habit's tick is saved with it in your account. The readings behind it are not. If you join a step challenge in Friends & challenges, we also use your daily step totals for that challenge's days to show you and the other people in it how everyone is pacing. Unless you turn Share usage data off (Settings → Help → Diagnostics), we also keep counts of which screens and features you use with your account, and look at totals of what people log in the app (such as how many meals and workouts, and which foods and exercises are most common), to see what to improve. Apple Health readings, and food or workouts that came from Apple Health, are never used for that. We never use consumer health data for advertising, never sell it, and neither we nor our processors use it to train AI models.

Your consent

Tempest asks for your consent before it collects any consumer health data. The consent screen appears when you set up the app, separate from the Terms and this policy, and you have to tick the box yourself. When this policy changes what we collect, the app asks again and says what changed. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Your data → Health data consent. When you withdraw, the app stops reading Apple Health and Motion & Fitness and stops syncing straight away, and asks again before collecting any more. Widgets stop showing your health numbers, booked health notifications are cleared, the coach turns off, and Coach's notes, your coach chats and your foods to avoid are cleared from your iPhone, and Delete my health data removes them from your account too. Step totals you shared in challenges are deleted, the health profile and body details backed up to your account are removed, and progress photos on your iPhone are deleted. We keep a record of when you give or withdraw consent (the date and time our server received it, the version of the consent and which you did), so we can show what you agreed to. Other data already in your account stays until you delete it, which you can do in Settings → Account → Delete account or by asking us.

We won't share consumer health data with anyone other than the processors listed below without asking for your separate consent first, and we will never sell it.

Sharing steps in challenges. Friends & challenges is off until you turn it on. The first time you join or create a step challenge, a separate screen asks whether you agree to share your daily step totals with the people in each challenge you join. It explains exactly what is shared, and nothing is shared until you agree. We store the version of that screen and the time you agreed with each challenge you join. To stop sharing, leave the challenge (its step totals are deleted unless another challenge of yours covers those days) or turn Friends & challenges off in Settings (all of them are deleted).

Who we share it with

We share consumer health data only with processors that run the app on our behalf, under contracts that limit them to doing that:

  • Supabase (with Amazon Web Services in the United States): stores your synced data (not your Apple Health readings) in your private account and runs sign-in.
  • Apple: Apple Health, Sign in with Apple and subscriptions run on Apple's systems, and Apple Maps draws the map under a workout route.
  • Anthropic (United States), only if you turn Coach on, after its own consent screen: to write Coach's answers and to read the food you log. With each question it receives your question and the recent chat; a summary of what you logged in the app (your food with its calories and macros for the last two weeks, the foods, recipes and saved meals you log most, your nutrition targets, goal and calorie floor, and this week's workouts with their exercises, sets and weights); a short summary of your Apple Health trends worked out on your iPhone (your average sleep, steps and training load over the last week, your weight trend unless you hide weight, today's Energy and call, last night's sleep, and this week's workouts from Apple Health: the kind, how long and how far, never a heart rate); and, if you set them, the foods you avoid, the notes Coach keeps about you, your care routines, your first name, and today's tasks, habits, goal and calendar events when you ask it to plan your day. With Coach on it also receives the food you log by typing or speaking, and photos of a meal or a nutrition label, to name each food. It never receives your other daily Apple Health readings, what is in your health profile, your email or your account id. Anthropic's terms bar it from training its models on any of it and require it to delete it within 30 days, unless it has to keep something longer to comply with the law or to investigate misuse of its service. Our server passes it through and keeps only counts and cost. We ask for your separate consent, on Coach's own screen, before any of it is sent.
  • OpenAI (United States), only when Coach reads an answer aloud in its own voice: the words of that answer, never your question, your data or who you are. OpenAI's terms bar training on them and limit how long they are kept to 30 days, with the same exceptions.
  • TelemetryDeck: counts how features are used. It receives a random id made for this install, never your account, name, email or any health reading. Some counts say that a health feature was used (for example that a weigh-in or a body measurement was saved, or roughly how many nights of data your first Energy score had), never the value and never which habit, experiment or body measurement it was. You can turn it off in Settings → Help → Diagnostics → Share usage data.

When Anthropic can't be reached, Apple's on-device model or the app's own rules write Coach's answer on your iPhone, and nothing is sent.

People you choose: the members of a step challenge you join see your daily step total for each of its days and the average worked out from them. They see nothing else from Apple Health, and no one outside the challenge sees your steps.

Your Apple Watch: if you install the Tempest Watch app, your iPhone sends it today's summary, your open workout, your routines and your recent foods directly. Nothing extra goes through our servers.

Food searches and barcode lookups go to Open Food Facts with only the words or barcode you entered, never any of your health data. We have no affiliates that receive consumer health data.

Your rights

  • Know and access: confirm whether we collect your consumer health data, see it, and get the list of every third party and affiliate we have shared it with (today, only the processors above). Settings → Your data → Export your data gives you a copy any time.
  • Withdraw consent: Settings → Your data → Health data consent.
  • Delete: Settings → Account → Delete account erases your account and all synced data at once. We also tell our processors to delete it, and it leaves our backups within 30 days. On the same screen you can choose to also remove what Tempest saved to Apple Health.
  • No discrimination: we won't treat you differently for using these rights.

To make a request by email, write to bigyeshllc@shatisand.com from the address on your account. We'll confirm it's you, respond within 45 days, and may extend that once by 45 days if we tell you why. If we decline, you can appeal by replying with "Appeal" in the subject; we'll answer within 45 days and explain why. If your appeal is denied, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General (atg.wa.gov), the Nevada Attorney General (ag.nv.gov), or the attorney general where you live.

How we protect it

Encryption in transit and at rest, database rules that let each account reach only its own data, and access limited to the people who need it to run the service. If a breach affects your health data, we'll tell you and the regulators the law requires.

Changes

If we change what consumer health data we collect or why, we'll update this policy and ask for your consent again in the app before collecting under the new terms.
