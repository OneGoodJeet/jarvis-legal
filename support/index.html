Help with the Tempest iPhone app

Contact us

Email bigyeshllc@shatisand.com. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened, and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days.

Subscription and free trial

How does the free trial work? The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial. You get every feature right away. When the trial ends, Apple charges the yearly price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

How do I cancel? On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Tempest. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Apple handles all billing, so we can't cancel or refund for you.

How do I get a refund? Request one from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I already pay but the app asks me to subscribe. On the subscription screen, tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Apple Health

Readiness or sleep is empty. Open the Health tab and tap Connect Apple Health. If you said no before, open the iPhone Settings app, then Health, then Data Access & Devices, then Tempest, and turn on the data you want to share.

Do I need an Apple Watch? No. Workouts, food, habits and tasks work on the iPhone alone. A watch or another wearable that writes sleep and heart data to Apple Health makes Readiness more accurate.

Your account and data

A new iPhone. Install Tempest and sign in with the same Apple ID. Your workouts, food, habits, tasks and goals sync back, and your health readings come back from Apple Health.

Export your data. In Tempest, tap the profile button on Today, then Settings, then Export your data. You get everything as JSON, or your workouts as CSV.

Delete your account. Tap the profile button on Today, then Settings, then Delete account. This deletes the account and all of its data from our servers and from your iPhone. It can't be undone. Apple Health keeps its own copy of your health data. Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription, so cancel it first in Settings, then Manage subscription.
